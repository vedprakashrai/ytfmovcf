This is the story of a rajput "Ashok kumar" marrying a tribal girl "Helen". This angers community's members. He is killed soon after the marriage by a Rajput king. The girl dies of shock. Angerd by their death, girl's brother kills the king and hides hides his body. The lovers take birth again after hundred years. But they cannot unite till the last rites of the king ...
