Parbat Pe Apna Dera
7 1944
Meera Devi played by Vanmala is a beautiful, rich heiress who is blind and seeks refuge with her father in Bhagwaan Shiva's temple. There she meets an "ascetic" Ram Babu played by Ullhas who has no worth for worldly pleasures or people, having devoted himself to worship of the god Shiva. Meera pleads with him to allow her to serve him and he takes pity on her and rest...
