The Murderer: Hamilton Palace
5.1 2011 108 min
Rocky is a famous dancer and singer at a top hotel, Hamilton Palace. News reporter Maheemaa goes missing after, in an unfortunate incident, she is flung from a building. Rocky is the main suspect as he had rejected Maheema's love for him. Maheema is shown deeply in love with him and after receiving the rejection, she promises Rocky that she will kill herself.
